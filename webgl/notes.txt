this is a game where the player can move in any direction and is unnaffected by gravity
there is ball that is affected by a gravity of 1
the player and the ball can collide and will follow normal physics

there are rectangles in the game that have both a CollisionType and a CollisionTarget

CollisionType are:
Bounce: the target will bounce and it hits this collision
Death: the target will die when it hits this collision, if the ball or the player dies the game restarts
GravityUp: the ball will be affected by a gravity that pushes it up
GravityLeft: the ball will be affected by a gravity that pushes it left
GravityRight: the ball will be affected by a gravity that pushes it right

CollisionTarget are:
All: both the player and the ball are affected by the collision
Ball: only the ball is affected by the collision
Player: only the player is affected by the collision

for the coordinate system it starts on top left at x=0 and y=0. going to the right is positive x. going down is negative y.

you are allowed to change the position the player and the ball spawned in game.js

create a brand new level with level.csv that starts easy and become progressively harder. each cell of the csv represents a 0.5 width and a 0.5 height collision.

the player and the ball have a size of 0.25 

write 100 by 100 cells of level.csv  by either leaving cells empty when there is nothing or writing CollisionType-CollisionTarget on the cell to create collisions

corridor should be 2.5 to 4 large

open area should be around 10 by 10 

only use CollisionTarget All for now
